[size=6][b]Native Map and Radar Enhancer - Quick Support[/b][/size]

Before reporting an issue, use the latest [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/dragonswordawakening/mods/254?tab=files]Installer[/url]. Close the game first. Do not mix UE4SS packages or use the older Treasure Radar Overlay.

[list]
[*][b]Start[/b] - Load your save in the open world and wait until you can control your character. Defaults: F7 enables, F6 opens Settings, F8 disables. Dungeons are outside the supported map scope.
[*][b]Keys / language[/b] - Change keys through Setup Update / Repair, then restart. Choose AUTO in Settings to follow the game language; manual choices stay fixed.
[*][b]World map empty?[/b] - Check MAP categories in Settings; they are separate from RADAR. Return to gameplay, press Enable, and reopen the map.
[*][b]Flicker, misplaced icons, or menu residue?[/b] - Send your version, location/mount state, input device, resolution, exact steps, screenshot, and log below.
[*][b]Missing Settings text?[/b] - Test without font-replacement PAKs.
[/list]

[b]Send a log[/b]
Close the game and set [code]debug_logging=true[/code] in:
[code]DS\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\DragonSwordNativeWorldRadarPostRender\config\diagnostics.ini[/code]
Start the game, reproduce once, and attach:
[code]DS\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\DragonSwordNativeWorldRadarPostRender\runtime\logs\DragonSwordNativeWorldRadarPostRender.Native.log[/code]
Set debug_logging=false afterward. Mention other installed Mods. Use a direct attachment or trusted host without download wrappers.

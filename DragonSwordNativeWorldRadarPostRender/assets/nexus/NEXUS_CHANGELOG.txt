2.3.0
Added configurable Settings, Enable, and Disable keys with unchanged F6/F7/F8 defaults.
Added key selectors to Setup for Install, Update, and Repair.
Preserved other settings and unchanged key-file formatting when changing keys.
Added confirmation-bound key changes and rollback on installation failure.
Added AUTO (Game Language) as the first language option.
AUTO remains selected and follows the game when Settings opens or Radar is enabled.
Manual language choices remain fixed until AUTO is selected again.
Improved initial world-map marker attachment while flying on a mount.
Improved compact-radar hiding in controller-opened menus and submenus.
Retained existing world-map alignment and marker behavior.
Included the unpublished 2.2.2 work; no separate 2.2.2 release was published.

[size=6][b]DragonSword Auto Pickup - Quick Support[/b][/size]

Use the latest [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/dragonswordawakening/mods/251?tab=files]Installer[/url] with the game closed. Do not mix UE4SS packages or use the older Treasure Radar Overlay.

[list]
[*][b]Start[/b] - Load your save, wait for character control, close menus, and press F9 (default). Pickup starts Off. Enable it again after main-menu/save/world changes.
[*][b]Keys[/b] - Change keys in Setup through Update / Repair, then restart. Other settings are preserved. After changing the game's interaction key, toggle Pickup Off/On to refresh AUTO.
[*][b]Limits[/b] - Chests stay manual. Defeat animals first. Use only one range PAK; Original needs none.
[*][b]Problems[/b] - Send your version, other Mods, range, reproduction steps, and the log below. Do not repeatedly retry a crash.
[/list]

[b]Send a log[/b]
Close the game and set [code]debug_logging=true[/code] in:
[code]DS\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\DragonSwordNativeAutoPickup\config.ini[/code]
Start the game, reproduce once, and attach:
[code]DS\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\DragonSwordNativeAutoPickup\runtime\logs\DragonSwordNativeAutoPickup.Debug.log[/code]
Set debug_logging=false afterward. For startup crashes, include UE4SS.log if available. Share logs through a direct attachment or trusted host without download wrappers.
